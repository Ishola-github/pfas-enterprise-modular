## One paragraph (primary)

**PFAS Enterprise 5.0** is a **research-use-only (RUO)** serum PFOS/PFOA **contextualization and screening scaffold** designed to **support laboratory-style governance** familiar from **ISO/IEC 17025-oriented** environments—manifest provenance, SHA-256 registry verification, schema-locked outputs, human reviewer sign-off, and applicability-domain refusal of out-of-scope inputs—**without** representing ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation, method validation, clinical diagnosis, or regulatory compliance. The frozen reproducibility release **`serum-v2.0.0-temporal`** (CI-verified commit `8ce2492`) is archived at **https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20348369** with companion assets at **https://github.com/Ishola-github/pfas-enterprise-modular/releases/tag/serum-v2.0.0-temporal**; independent blind reproduction is in progress under a fixed-scope pilot (≥2 external PASS attestations required before “externally reproducible” promotion). The platform is **decision-support infrastructure**, not an automated compliance engine or substitute for accredited analytical methods (e.g., EPA 533/537.1/1633 where applicable).
