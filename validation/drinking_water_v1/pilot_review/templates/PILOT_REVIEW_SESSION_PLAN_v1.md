## Reviewer Tasks

1. Identify highest-priority rows for manual follow-up.
2. Explain uncertainty and applicability messaging for a selected row.
3. Identify one value-add and one limitation.

## Success Signals

- Reviewer can interpret prioritization output without ad hoc explanation.
- Reviewer understands limits and does not confuse output with compliance decisioning.
- Reviewer reports meaningful workflow benefit (time saved, triage clarity, reporting clarity).
