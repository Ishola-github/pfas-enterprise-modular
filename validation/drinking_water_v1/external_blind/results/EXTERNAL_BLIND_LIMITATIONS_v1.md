# External Blind Limitations Statement (v1)

Use/adapt this text in external blind reporting.

## Required Conservative Scope

This evaluation supports evidence for a screening / prioritization / governance platform.

## Prohibited Overclaims

Do not claim:

- accredited
- certified analytical method
- regulatory-approved decision engine
- compliance automation
- replacement for wet-lab LC-MS/MS analytical workflows

## Scientific Boundaries

- External blind performance may vary by matrix, method context, and data quality.
- Applicability-domain warnings must be treated as material evidence.
- Prediction outputs are decision-support artifacts, not standalone compliance determinations.

## Regulatory Boundary

This external blind evidence package does not itself establish EPA approval, ISO 17025 accreditation, CLIA validation, or prospective field validation sufficiency for regulated release.
