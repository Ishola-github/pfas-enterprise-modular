PFAS Enterprise 5.0 — Serum contextualization demo package v1
INTENDED USE (Research Use Only)

This demo package demonstrates governed population-reference contextualization
of serum PFOS/PFOA measurements against weighted CDC NHANES reference distributions,
with optional cross-cycle temporal comparison across NHANES cycles I, J, and P.

PERMITTED USES:
- Research and method evaluation
- Environmental-health analytics pilots
- Consulting and academic collaboration demonstrations
- Reproducibility and governance audits
- Grant or SBIR technical appendices (with RUO labeling)

PROHIBITED REPRESENTATIONS:
- Clinical diagnosis or medical decision-making
- Individual patient risk classification for treatment
- EPA regulatory compliance or legal defensibility claims
- ISO/IEC 17025 laboratory accreditation of the software
- Replacement for certified analytical laboratory results

V2 temporal outputs compare POPULATION reference percentiles across survey cycles.
They do NOT constitute individual longitudinal medical follow-up.

All outputs must retain manifest JSON and SHA-256 provenance when cited externally.
