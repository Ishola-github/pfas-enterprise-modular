Below-LOD semantics
- NHANES imputes values below the limit of detection at
  LOD / sqrt(2).
- Each analyte concentration column is paired with an LOD
  comment column (LBD*L in the raw XPT, lbd*l after
  janitor::clean_names): 0 = at/above LOD, 1 = below LOD.
- Statistics that depend on the censored portion (any
  percentile near or below LOD, any "fraction detected"
  metric) MUST consult the paired LOD column.

----------------------------------------------------------------
Refusal conditions (the lane MUST refuse the input)
----------------------------------------------------------------

R1. Input not derived from human serum biomonitoring.
R2. Cycle / population beyond NHANES J coverage, unless paired
    with a documented harmonized cross-cycle artifact. The
    Pre-pandemic (P_PFAS) and 2003-2004 (L06AGE_C) cycles are
    NOT covered by v1.0 and require an explicit v1.1 or v2.0
    AD revision.
R3. Analyte not present in the 9-member panel above.
R4. Reported concentration outside the observed envelope by
    more than 3 sigma (relative to the cycle J distribution
    for that analyte) without supporting batch metadata.
R5. Mixed-matrix records (e.g., a row that simultaneously
    carries a drinking-water concentration and a serum
    concentration). Cross-matrix harmonization requires its
    own artifact in a separate directory.
R6. Units other than ng/mL without an explicit, documented
    unit-conversion transformation in the ingestion pipeline.

----------------------------------------------------------------
Out-of-scope, explicitly
----------------------------------------------------------------

- Soil / sediment, fish / tissue, or any other environmental
  matrix.
- Cross-cycle merging of NHANES J with NHANES C (2003-2004)
  or the Pre-pandemic combined release (P_PFAS).
- Clinical individual diagnosis. Population biomonitoring
  reference ranges are not individual reference intervals.
- Source-attribution claims (which contamination source
  contributed which fraction of a measured serum burden).

This applicability domain is anchored only to the 2017-2018
NHANES cycle. Any expansion to additional cycles or
populations must be issued as a new versioned artifact
(serum_v1.1 or serum_v2) and reviewed against the matrix-
isolation requirement in this same directory.
