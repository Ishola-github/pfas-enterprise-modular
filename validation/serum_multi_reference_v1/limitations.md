# Limitations — multi-reference comparison (v1)

- Reference layers use **different surveys and harmonization rules**; deltas are
  contextual, not causal.
- ATSDR and HBM4EU lanes are **not active** until raw data are acquired, pinned,
  and harmonized per lane SOPs.
- Race/ethnicity categories may not map 1:1 across NHANES, ATSDR, and EU surveys.
- Percentiles are population-referenced; they do not identify exposure source.
- UCMR5 environmental data must not be interpreted as serum body-burden percentiles.
