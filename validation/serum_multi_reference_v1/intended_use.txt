PFAS Enterprise 5.0 — Multi-reference percentile comparison (RUO)

INTENDED USE
- Research and operational support for comparing serum PFAS results against
  multiple pinned population reference layers (NHANES baseline; ATSDR exposed
  communities when available; HBM4EU when harmonized).
- Manifest-backed, reproducible cross-reference deviation reporting.

NOT INTENDED FOR
- Clinical diagnosis, treatment decisions, or individual medical risk classification.
- Regulatory compliance determination or litigation outcome prediction.
- Automated disease or health-outcome inference.

All outputs require qualified human review and RUO labeling.
