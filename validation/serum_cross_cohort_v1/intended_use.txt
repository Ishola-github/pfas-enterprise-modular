PFAS Enterprise 5.0 — Serum cross-cohort comparator (v1 scaffold)

Intended use (RUO):
  - Compare governed cohort summary outputs across cohorts using a common
    contextualization contract.
  - Quantify subgroup deltas against NHANES baseline context.
  - Support external-validation analytics workflows with manifest-backed outputs.

Not intended for:
  - Clinical diagnosis
  - Regulatory compliance determination
  - Causal inference claims
  - Silent pooling of incompatible cohort schemas
