PFAS Enterprise 5.0 — Serum lane ATSDR scaffold (v1)

Intended use (RUO):
  - External validation context comparing exposed-community serum PFAS
    distributions against NHANES population-reference outputs.
  - Research and consulting workflows for cohort-level exposure intelligence.
  - Governance and reproducibility demonstrations using manifest + SHA controls.

Not intended for:
  - Clinical diagnosis or individual treatment decisions.
  - Regulatory compliance determination or legal certification.
  - Silent pooling with NHANES reference engines or environmental matrix lanes.

ATSDR lane outputs must preserve provenance metadata and remain explicitly
labeled as non-NHANES cohort contextualization.
