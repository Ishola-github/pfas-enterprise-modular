Lane:      serum (human biomonitoring)
Version:   v2.0 (cross-cycle temporal contextualization)
Builds on: V1.1 ontology + nhanes_pfas_weighted_reference_tables_v1_1.csv

----------------------------------------------------------------
In-domain inputs
----------------------------------------------------------------

All V1.1 in-domain requirements, plus:

reference_cycle (required for V2)
  - One of: I, J, P
  - Selects anchor cycle for primary percentile and delta baseline
  - Must appear in reference_cycles_available in the V2 ontology

Comparison cycles (fixed in V2.0)
  - I  = NHANES 2015-2016  (PFAS_I,  WTSB2YR)
  - J  = NHANES 2017-2018  (PFAS_J,  WTSB2YR)
  - P  = NHANES 2017-2020  (P_PFAS,  WTSBAPRP, pre-pandemic)

Analytes (same as V1)
  - n_pfoa, sb_pfoa, n_pfos, sm_pfos

----------------------------------------------------------------
Out of scope
----------------------------------------------------------------

- Cycle H (2013-2014) — isomer columns in SSPFAS_H; not harmonized to V1 four-isomer panel
- Individual repeat measures / clinic longitudinal series
- Environmental matrices
- PFHxS, PFNA, and extended PFAS panel (V3+ ontology scope)
