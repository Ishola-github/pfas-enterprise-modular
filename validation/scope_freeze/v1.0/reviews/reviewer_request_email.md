# Outreach Email Templates — Independent Scope Review

Three variants below, one short follow-up. Customize the `[bracketed]`
fields with real names, real paper / area references, and your own
sign-off. The variants are deliberately short — cold emails over a few
hundred words get deleted unread.

## Subject lines (pick one)

- `Brief: independent scope review request — PFAS prototype (2-4 hrs, pro-bono)`
- `Independent reviewer request — PFAS screening scope document`
- `Request for technical scope review — PFAS multi-matrix prototype`

## What NOT to put in the email

| Don't say | Why |
| --- | --- |
| "Will you endorse my platform?" | Wrong frame; the ask is scope **review**, not endorsement. |
| "Will you advise our company?" | Implies an ongoing relationship; this is a single review. |
| "Will you be on our advisory board?" | Premature and materially changes the ask. |
| Mention of SBIR funding, future revenue, equity, or commercial benefit | Pro-bono is pro-bono — bringing money into the conversation undermines the framing. |
| Attachments | Link to GitHub instead. Reviewer reads on whatever device they like. |
| Long backstory about the platform | Brief is the brief. The email is the hook. |

## Honest expectations on response rate

- Cold outreach to specific researchers / consultants: 10-25% reply rate is normal.
- Of those who reply, ~50% will say "no/not a fit/forward to X."
- Of those who agree, expect 2-4 weeks from "yes" to filled-in checklist.
- Send 5-10 outreach emails for a realistic shot at 1-2 completed reviews.

Plan accordingly. This is not engineering — it is patient relationship work.
