This dataset is the cycle-H human serum biomonitoring lane of
PFAS Enterprise 5.0, anchored to the NHANES 2013-2014 PFAS Serum
Special Subsample (NHANES cycle H, dataset file PFAS_H.XPT).

It is intended to support analyst review of population-level
PFAS exposure ranges, distributional summaries, and
applicability-domain boundary checks specific to the cycle-H
serum lane.

It is a peer of the cycle-J serum lane (validation/serum_v1/),
NOT a successor. The two lanes cover different analyte panels
(cycle H adds PFBS, PFHpA, and PFDoA; cycle H lacks the
isomer-resolved n-/Sb- PFOA and n-/Sm- PFOS columns that cycle J
carries; the cycle-H isomer split lives in the companion file
SSPFAS_H.XPT, which is recorded but not admitted here -- see
schema_contract.md sec 7.1).

It does not replace clinical laboratory testing, individual
toxicokinetic assessment, occupational biomonitoring
surveillance programs, or any form of regulatory exposure
determination.

It is also not a tool for environmental contamination source
attribution. Human serum measurements describe internal
exposure, not the source matrices (drinking water,
biosolids, AFFF, methanol standards, air emissions) that
contributed to it.

It must not be combined, on a per-row basis, with the cycle-J
serum lane (data/training/serum/nhanes_serum_pfas_2017_2018.csv,
SHA-256 dfd4dbb5...4490f) without an explicit cross-cycle
harmonization artifact, because the analyte panels differ. The
two anchors are independent.

This dataset is human biomonitoring data. It must not be
combined with environmental-matrix datasets without an
explicit cross-matrix harmonization artifact, because each
matrix carries its own analytical method, unit system, and
regulatory framing. The matrix-isolation requirement is
enforced by the schema contract and the applicability domain
in this directory.

Note on units: cycle H's SAS variable labels read "(ug/L)" while
the codebook's LLOD table is explicitly in "ng/mL". The two unit
strings denote the same numerical scale within method tolerance
(1 ug/L = 1 ng/mL for aqueous matrix; serum density ~1.025 g/mL
accounts for the residual ~2.5%). The anchor CSV records the
values exactly as NHANES published them and the contract
declares them as ng/mL. Treating them as anything else without
the reconciliation step in schema_contract.md sec 3.2 is a
governance violation.
