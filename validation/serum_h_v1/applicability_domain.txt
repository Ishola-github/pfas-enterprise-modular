Below-LOD semantics
- NHANES imputes values below the limit of detection at
  LOD / sqrt(2). With cycle H's constant 0.10 ng/mL LLOD, the
  imputed value is ~0.0707 ng/mL for every below-LOD row.
- Each analyte concentration column is paired with an LOD
  comment column (LBD*L in the raw XPT, lbd*l after
  janitor::clean_names): 0 = at/above LOD, 1 = below LOD.
- Statistics that depend on the censored portion (any
  percentile near or below LOD, any "fraction detected"
  metric) MUST consult the paired LOD column. Five of the
  eight cycle-H analytes (PFDeA, MPAH, PFBS, PFHpA, PFUnDA,
  PFDoA) are dominated by the imputed value.

Label-unit reconciliation (cycle-H specific)
- The SAS variable labels in PFAS_H.XPT annotate every value
  column with "(ug/L)".
- The CDC online codebook simultaneously documents the LLOD
  table in "ng/mL" and treats the two units as equivalent for
  aqueous matrices.
- 1 ug/L equals 1 ng/mL for aqueous matrix to within ~2.5%
  (serum density ~1.025 g/mL accounts for the residual).
- The anchor CSV records numerical values exactly as NHANES
  published them, with no unit conversion applied.
- The lane contract declares the units as ng/mL because the
  codebook's LLOD table does so, the numbers agree with ng/mL
  to within method tolerance, and the same numbers in cycle J
  are labelled (ng/mL).
- Double conversion (relabel as ug/L AND multiply by a factor)
  is refused under condition R6 below.

----------------------------------------------------------------
Refusal conditions (the lane MUST refuse the input)
----------------------------------------------------------------

R1. Input not derived from human serum biomonitoring.
R2. Source cycle is not NHANES H (2013-2014). The cycle-J
    (PFAS_J), cycle-I (PFAS_I), Pre-pandemic (P_PFAS),
    cycle-C (L06AGE_C), and any other NHANES cycle are NOT
    covered by serum_h v1.0 and require their own versioned
    lanes. Cycle J is already governed separately by
    validation/serum_v1/.
R3. Analyte not present in the 8-member panel above.
R4. Reported concentration outside the observed envelope by
    more than 3 sigma (relative to the cycle-H distribution
    for that analyte) without supporting batch metadata.
R5. Mixed-matrix records (e.g., a row that simultaneously
    carries a drinking-water concentration and a serum
    concentration). Cross-matrix harmonization requires its
    own artifact in a separate directory.
R6. Units other than ng/mL without explicit reconciliation
    against schema_contract.md sec 3.2. Double conversion
    (relabel ug/L AND multiply) is never authorized.
R7. The row claims an analyte from the isomer-resolved
    PFOA/PFOS panel (n-PFOA, Sb-PFOA, n-PFOS, Sm-PFOS)
    without SSPFAS_H.XPT being co-admitted. Cycle H stores
    the isomer split in a separate file; silently filling
    those columns from PFAS_H.XPT alone is a contract
    violation.
R8. Cross-cycle merging with serum (cycle J) on a per-row
    basis without a documented harmonization artifact.

----------------------------------------------------------------
Out-of-scope, explicitly
----------------------------------------------------------------

- Soil / sediment, fish / tissue, or any other environmental
  matrix.
- Cross-cycle merging of NHANES H with NHANES J (cycle-J is
  governed by validation/serum_v1/, frozen).
- Cross-cycle merging of NHANES H with NHANES C (2003-2004),
  NHANES I (2015-2016), or the Pre-pandemic combined release
  (P_PFAS).
- The isomer-resolved companion file SSPFAS_H.XPT itself is
  RECORDED in this directory's provenance.md but NOT admitted
  under this anchor. Admission requires a follow-up artifact
  (see schema_contract.md sec 7.1).
- Clinical individual diagnosis. Population biomonitoring
  reference ranges are not individual reference intervals.
- Source-attribution claims (which contamination source
  contributed which fraction of a measured serum burden).

This applicability domain is anchored only to the 2013-2014
NHANES cycle H. Any expansion to additional cycles or
populations must be issued as a new versioned artifact
(serum_h_v1.1 or serum_h_v2) and reviewed against the
matrix-isolation and cross-cycle-isolation requirements in
this same directory.
